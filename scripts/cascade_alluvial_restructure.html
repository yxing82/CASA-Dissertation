Aggregate cascade flows by IMD rank decile — showing inflow from wealthier areas (higher-ranked) and outflow to poorer areas (lower-ranked), across both census periods.

Reading the pattern

Deciles 1–5 (most deprived) show net positive cascade — more people arriving from wealthier areas than leaving to poorer ones, consistent with ascent-dominated restructuring. Deciles 6–10 show the mirror: net negative cascade indicating displacement-dominated dynamics. The symmetry pivot around Decile 5 is the clearest fingerprint of the cascading displacement mechanism, persisting across both census periods.

Data: ONS Census O-D 2011 & 2021 · IMD 2010 & 2019 (MHCLG) · 983 London MSOAs · Deciles from mean LSOA IMD score per MSOA (1 = most deprived, 10 = least deprived) · Regime: CFI Churn (median) × Net Cascade (quartiles), averaged across both O-D periods
